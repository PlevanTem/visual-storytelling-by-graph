FIT & EXPECTATIONS

Know what to bring.
Know what to expect.

Data with enough meaning

Tables and time series support everyday questions. Pairing, intervals, compositions and paths need their corresponding data. Units, denominators and sampling rules matter.

An output for the task

Expect a suitable chart or table, findings and important limits. Interactive, editable or exportable outputs depend on your Agent's available tools.

Claims the data can support

Missing evidence stays explicit. A before/after change is not proof of causality. When a key input is absent, the result should explain what is needed.

THE DECISIONS BEHIND THE EXPRESSION

A method you can follow.

The intended advantage is a repeatable design process.
Each layer answers a different question.

  1. 01

    Intent

    Who needs to understand what, and for which decision?

  2. 02

    Data meaning

    Preserve units, denominators and pairing. A total and a rate answer different questions.

  3. 03

    Expression

    Keep distributions for tail questions; compare alternatives against the task.

  4. 04

    Reading & interaction

    Make the finding visible first. Add filters or detail only when they help the reader.

  5. 05

    Checks & revision

    Do the calculations, marks, display and operations agree?

Inspectable examples and four independent specialist Agent runs support the implementation. Comparative gains, human comprehension and aesthetic preference have not been measured.
